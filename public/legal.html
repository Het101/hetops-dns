Terms of service

The service

HetOps DNS checks the public DNS, TLS, email-authentication and web configuration of domains. Scans are free. Domain Watch plans (Free, Pro, Team) add monitoring, alerts and automatic DMARC report collection, as described on the pricing.

Acceptable use

  • Only add domains to monitoring, or point DMARC reports at us, for domains you own or are allowed to manage.
  • Don't use the service or API to attack, overload or probe systems you aren't authorised to test, and stay within the published rate limits.
  • We may suspend accounts that break these rules.

Paid plans

You can cancel any time from Manage billing in your account; you keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for, then the account returns to Free. Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Scans: the domain you scan. Scans without an account are not linked to you; recent scans are kept in your own browser.
  • Account: your email address (for sign-in links and alerts), the domains you watch, your alert settings and change history.
  • DMARC reports: if you use automatic collection, the aggregate reports mail providers send about your domain. These list sending IP addresses and message counts, not message content.
  • Billing: your plan and subscription status. Card and payment details go to Lemon Squeezy and never reach us.
  • Usage: cookieless, aggregate page analytics (self-hosted Umami) with no personal identifiers, plus standard server logs kept for security and debugging.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Who processes it

Lemon Squeezy (payments), our email provider (sign-in and alert emails), Cloudflare (DNS and DMARC email routing) and Oracle Cloud (hosting). We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires.

Retention and your rights

Account data is kept while your account exists. Change history and DMARC reports are capped (the most recent 500 alerts; DMARC reports up to 365 days). You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account and everything in it by emailing patel.x.het@gmail.com; we act within 30 days.
